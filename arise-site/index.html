FAQ

Does onboarding matter?

Yes — goal, location, equipment, level and days re-sort recommendations deterministically. Minimal kit pushes beginner-friendly first.

Is there a nutrition system?

Intentionally not. That recreates Forq and distracts from the training proposition.

Can I install it?

Yes — Add to Home Screen, then airplane-mode reload keeps Today/Exercises/schedule from cache.
