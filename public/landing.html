A logger, a coach, and an evidence engine — in that order

First, logging must be effortless between sets. Second, the coach must prescribe the next session with a stated reason. Third — only ever third — the evidence engine tells you how much to trust that coach. The order is the product.

Logger

One thumb, sweaty hands

Gym Mode: large targets, swipe to complete or fail, equipment-aware quick-add, rest timer with sound and haptics. Logging never gets in the way of training.

Coach

Every number carries a reason

Double progression with conservative defaults, plate-math honesty, sustained-trend deloads, guardrails against overshoot. Tap any prescription to see the rule and the evidence.

Evidence engine

Honest about uncertainty

Recommendations frozen before each workout, scored against what actually happened. Confidence bands on charts. Conclusions withheld until sample sizes earn them.

Trust is a feature

  • No account, no server. Local-first: your history lives in your browser, on your device.
  • Export everything, any time. Open formats, optional encryption, no lock-in mechanism exists.
  • Telemetry off by default. Optional local measurements are consent-gated and never leave the device.
  • Open source, MIT. Audit the engine, not just the copy.
  • Sync only if you want it. Bring your own WebDAV storage, end-to-end encrypted. No Arise server exists.
“What Arise cannot prove” is a page in the docs, not a fine print: training logs cannot measure muscle growth, and this app will not pretend otherwise.

Install in under a minute

Arise is a PWA: install it and it runs standalone, offline, with its own icon. Android and desktop install in one tap from the app; iPhone uses Safari’s Add to Home Screen.
