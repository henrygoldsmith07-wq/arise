# Disclaimers

The short statements below are the canonical wording; the app surfaces them
in context (onboarding, recommendations, AI insight, exports), and this page
is the full reference.

## Medical disclaimer

Arise is **not a medical device** and does not provide medical advice,
diagnosis, or treatment. Progression suggestions, readiness scores, deload
recommendations, and AI-generated summaries are arithmetic and heuristics
over the data you log — they are not healthcare guidance. Consult a qualified
health professional before starting or changing an exercise program,
especially if you are pregnant, injured, or managing a medical condition.

## Safety disclaimer for high-intensity recommendations

Where Arise suggests higher loads, reduced rest, or pushing close to failure,
these are extrapolations from your own logged performance, not assessments
of your current capacity. Technique breakdown, fatigue, sleep debt, illness,
and medication all change what is safe on a given day. Warm up, use a spotter
or safeties for maximal attempts, and treat every suggestion as a question to
answer with your own judgment — never as an instruction. Stop immediately if
you feel sharp pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.

## Age appropriateness

Arise is a general-audience tool and is **not directed at children under
13**. Users aged 13–17 should train under qualified coaching supervision,
with loads guided by a professional — developing athletes should not chase
adult-style progressive overload numbers, and Arise's suggestions do not
account for growth-stage considerations. There is no age-gate because there
is nothing to gate: the app collects no data from anyone.

## Data ownership

Your training data is **yours**. It lives in your browser on your device;
Arise claims no license over it, will never sell or analyze it, and cannot
read it. You can export everything at any time (`More → Data → Export JSON`,
CSV for exercise history), and clearing local data deletes it completely.
If you keep backups in your own cloud storage via sync, ownership and access
are governed by your agreement with that provider — the data remains yours.
